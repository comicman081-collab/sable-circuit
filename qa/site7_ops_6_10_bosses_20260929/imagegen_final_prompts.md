# 선택 원본 ImageGen 요청 전문

도구: Codex 내장 `image_gen.imagegen`. 각 요청은 `transparent_background: true`로 보냈다. 아래는 선택 원본을 만든 요청의 `prompt` 전문이다. 시도 횟수와 거절 사유는 `imagegen_attempts.json`에 있다.

## AERATOR TOWER — attempt 03

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent PNG game source master. background: transparent_alpha. Produce ONE AERATOR TOWER stationary mushroom-shaped hydroponic boss machine for SABLE CIRCUIT. Premium 2.5D stylized realistic hard-surface art, fixed dimetric near-orthographic three-quarter top-down camera. Large rounded armoured pressure-vessel cap over a thick short stem, two unequal planter pods and hoses below cap, wide octagonal bolted base. Exactly one lime-green omnidirectional emitter flower-bud at cap crown with five angular petal louvres. White and graphite weathered armour, lime status lights, neutral white light from upper left. NO humanoid limbs or face, no tracks, wheels, gun barrel, iris, concentric rings or portal. COMPOSITION OVERRIDES ALL OTHER FRAMING: illustrate this entire machine SMALL in the CENTER of a large square transparent canvas. The machine, including glow and floor base, should occupy only the CENTRAL 60% of the canvas HEIGHT and at most 65% of canvas WIDTH. Leave a very large completely blank transparent border, at least 20% of total canvas dimensions on ALL FOUR SIDES. Do not create stray glow, shadow, ground dust or any nonzero alpha in that empty border. This master will be scaled later for game display, so the generous native margin is intentional. Genuine RGBA with background alpha exactly 0 and opaque interior alpha 255, no painted checkerboard or green. No floor, shadow plate, backdrop, scenery, text, symbols, UI, extra subjects, explosions, smoke or projectiles. Never crop, key, clamp or paint the alpha; output native transparent generation. Longest source dimension at least 1024 pixels.
```
