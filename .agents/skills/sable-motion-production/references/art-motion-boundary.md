# Appearance stays with ImageGen; motion stays with Blender/UAL

The user corrected a real scope failure on 2026-09-07. The anatomical pilot
rebuilt face/eyes, scalp/braid, coat and boots from geometry and constant
materials. Attaching approved face/cloth pixels did not make the result an
ImageGen character. Exact hashes and a closed mesh cannot grant that authority.
Its actual first native image failed likeness, duplicate features, costume and
footwear. Preserve the failed bundle in quarantine; never repair that visual
reconstruction recipe into another production candidate.

Permitted local work: bones, weights and deformation of approved ImageGen
pixels/layers, pose transfer, licensed UAL retargeting, ground/stride/cadence
calibration, contact measurement, pose-guide export, packaging of already
approved ImageGen motion frames, and Godot input/aim/fire integration.
Non-character rig/control diagnostics may continue independently. They are not
visible character assets or ground truth for the rendered character's soles.
Deterministic background-only normalization is allowed only when it proves all
retained subject pixels are byte-identical and then receives edge visual review.
It cannot be used to erase, redraw, restore, or inpaint character pixels.

Every final visible character pixel must follow one of two routes: it is an
unchanged/deformed pixel from hashed approved ImageGen art, or it comes from a
new built-in ImageGen pose request that used the Blender/UAL result only as a
motion guide and then passed identity/costume review. A Blender/UAL render of a
generic, VRoid, or locally reconstructed avatar may be diagnostic pose evidence,
but it is never character art and can never enter an atlas or runtime pointer.

Not permitted: a new Blender face/hair/costume/boot/weapon, procedural material
replacement, generic VRoid/CC0 appearance presented as MICA, restoration of
missing illustration pixels, or reusing a tiny approved patch over every face.
Missing visible regions or poses require an ImageGen source request scoped to
that missing input, with identity/costume approval. Do not regenerate good art
merely because executable approval became stale.

`tools/character_pipeline/art_authority_policy.json` lists retired paths/content
and exact independently reviewed motion-only adapters. The production adapter
list is intentionally empty until an actual implementation preserves source
appearance. Adding `art_authority: imagegen_visuals_blender_motion_only` and
allowed operation labels is necessary but insufficient; full executable closure
and real evidence/reviews are required. No fake registration to bypass HOLD.

Before any next candidate: prove neutral visual preservation against the exact
approved image, trace every actual visible pixel to approved ImageGen art,
and reject replacement appearance or missing/duplicate parts. Then validate
one actual movement clip; do not inherit motion approval from static identity.
No return to rejected calf-plane rotations, arbitrary pixel offsets or taped-on
rectangular coat patches. Keep the existing real stride, contact, 8-direction,
8x8 aim/fire, 30/60/120Hz, web and same-runtime HTML requirements.

Record source/art approval separately from local adapter correctness. Historical
reviews are preserved; current code hashes require renewed affected reviews,
not edited old PASS receipts. Astra actual success must still precede Luna.
