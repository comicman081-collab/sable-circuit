# SABLE CIRCUIT — integrated local demo

## Player flow

Title → Start Demo → existing silent 60-second intro → Operations → Briefing →
combat/exploration/extraction → Results → Operations/refit/next mission.
Continue to Operations bypasses the intro; Watch Intro returns to Title.
The five training buttons are isolated encounters and grant no persistent reward.

Connected existing gameplay: three current authored operators at 1.8x scale,
five campaign missions, robot-only enemy roster and bosses, physical cover,
shooting/reload/evade, Q/E/X skills and squad synergy, operator switching,
followers, held-F revive, optional rooms, extraction and wipe transactions,
research/salvage/intel, Armory and Lab upgrades, weapon/module unlocks and
equipment, existing save-schema migration and persistence, battle SFX and credits.
No new humanoid enemies, reset of player saves or fabricated unlocks.

## Controls

- WASD or arrows: move. Shift: run. Mouse: aim. Left click: fire.
- R: reload. Space: evade. Q/E/X: skills. 1/2/3: change operator.
- F: interact/extract; hold to revive. C: continue past extraction window.
- H or Escape: pause/manual. Resume, leave training, or confirm campaign abort
  (uses the normal wipe rules, not a free extraction).
- Touch: left movement pad, right aim/fire pad; simultaneous touch IDs are
  independent. Two rows of large action buttons expose the same gameplay paths.
  Touch mode automatically enables on a touchscreen or the first touch.
  Releasing a finger, losing focus or leaving the scene clears held input.

Run locally with tools/environment/start_demo.ps1. Current native verification
uses installed Godot 4.7.1 and isolated QA saves, not the player's save.
