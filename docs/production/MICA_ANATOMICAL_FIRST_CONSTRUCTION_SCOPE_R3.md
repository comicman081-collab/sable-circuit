# MICA anatomical neutral construction — limited first-pose scope

This authorizes no render on its own. The exact file, builder, runner, source
receipt and inputs must be bound in an independently reviewed build plan.

## What is constructed

One S-facing neutral, empty-handed pose, native 1920x1920. The approved S
ImageGen master remains unchanged. No ImageGen retry, local raster generation,
motion frames, weapon/combat approval, current-pointer change or packaging.

Use the licensed Blender Studio female realistic base and its project-local R4
rig diagnostic as anatomical inputs, not as appearance authority. Import only
the actual body, its two eyeballs and anatomical armature. Clear diagnostic
animation; do not load/render the rest of that diagnostic scene. Preserve the
closed underlying body, normalized actual skin weights and genuine legs.

Fit jacket/scalp/boot surfaces to actual anatomical faces. Make a continuous
curved split coat with matching entire waist seams and separate teal lining.
Boots are reconstructed as a genuine enclosing 3mm voxel isosurface without volume-preservation reprojection before final skinning and sole vertex selection. Body/leg
geometry must not be replaced by cylinders, billboards, limb warps or hidden
sole helpers. Piping, braid strands and manufactured equipment are geometry,
not procedurally generated raster source art.

The approved conservative coat patch is a repeatable material swatch, not a
complete body texture. The approved face region is projected only on actual
front anatomical face surface inside that mask. No source hand, leg silhouette,
green backdrop or unapproved region may be used as a garment texture.
Remaining fitted surfaces use ordinary constant Blender materials. The first
pose must still show whether this reconstruction actually preserves MICA's
likeness, detailed costume, fitted cloth, covered anatomy and neutral boots.
Code/UV checks cannot certify that appearance. Do not approve an obvious generic
body, hair/face mismatch, featureless costume or missing equipment merely
because it is closed geometry. A failed first pose stops production expansion.

## Process boundary

Reserve one exact build attempt. The runner must atomically claim it once before
creating a fresh output root. The Blender child must verify that runner claim
and claim its own single execution before constructing anything. Duplicate
claims, a used output root, changed input bytes or changed direction are errors.
Both claims become first-pose evidence. A crashed attempt remains spent.

One hidden owned Blender child, 2 CPU threads, at most 240 seconds. TEMP, TMP,
application data, caches, logs, scene, image and all records stay within the
approved project output root. No loops, GPU background job or Godot export.
The normal runner checks child exit, original input hashes, mesh preflight,
decoded first-pose image and exact construction binding before completion.
`JOB_COMPLETE` means construction finished, **not visual PASS**.

## Not included

Weapon reconstruction/aim, any UAL bake, all other viewing directions, complete
walk/run contact, 8x8 fire, runtime validation, HTML, web review and Luna
reproduction all remain subsequent gated work. An empty-handed neutral source
cannot pass a combat-pose muzzle check; the next stage must explicitly distinguish
neutral-rig approval from weapon/combat approval instead of declaring a visible
muzzle that does not exist.


## R3 construction correction: measured shoe envelope

R2 failed before rendering because cuff closure inverted the right boot shell.
The original bare-foot toe quads also fold under a simple 9mm normal offset;
component winding correction alone is insufficient. Construct a new closed
Blender voxel envelope (.003m, adaptivity 0, preserve_volume false), never project
it back into those folds. Keep both full-size left/right shoe surfaces.
Only disconnected 8-vertex/6-face subvoxel cells, each at most .003m in every
axis, may be removed during construction. Record exact removed cell geometry,
bounds, IDs and signed volumes. This is topology authoring, not asset disposal.
Do not remove any full-size shell or attached boot part.

Verify final two outward closed components, all triangle degeneracy/folding,
BVH plus independent shared-vertex/edge intersection candidates and narrow-phase
intersection outside allowed shared boundaries. Record the numerical tolerance.
Verify actual source foot/covered calf vertices below .40m lie inside the final
surface. The top cuff transition at and above .40m is explicitly excluded from
that foot-enclosure test, never nominated as a sole. No anatomy alpha erasure.
Use side-specific closed-shell parity from three non-axis rays, at least two
unambiguous agreeing results; edge-hit/contradicting parity means HOLD. A single
nearest-face normal is not inside/outside authority. Surface proximity has a
1e-5m numerical boundary tolerance, not a hidden allowance for penetration.
Removed detached subvoxel cells must also have absolute signed volume <=1e-9m3.

Transfer skin by actual nearest anatomical triangle and normalized barycentric
weights; record each final vertex's source triangle IDs and coefficients. Every
outer-side surface must remain within .025m of the anatomical source; the inward
closing top cap can be farther from the original hollow calf surface. Record
both maxima separately, with cap classification z>.40m and upward normal>.5.
Select entirely new sole IDs only from the final actual downward boot surface.
Do not preserve the provisional offset mesh's sole IDs or source vertex mapping.

These geometric repairs require exact implementation/Ponytail plan review and
one bounded native visual review; they do not constitute appearance or motion
approval. New helpers and actual diagnostic evidence are part of the new plan.


## R4 construction correction: resolved facial overlay and stable image paths

R3 built the anatomical mesh and shoe envelope but failed before rendering:
45 source-projected loop triangles were below the unchanged minimum absolute
UV cross product times source width/height of 0.25 (double area in pixels;
triangle area 0.125 px2). The 1536 coat triangles were valid.

Triangulate only the separate front facial overlay, then omit unresolved
triangles whose source projection is below that existing threshold. In the
actual diagnostic, BEAUTY triangulation yielded 46 omitted triangles and 1358
retained triangles. These include near-tangent surfaces and truly tiny faces;
do not claim they were all outside the approved mask. Do not stretch UVs,
change source pixels, erase anatomy, or relax the collector threshold.
Underlying opaque anatomical body/head coordinates, topology and weights
must be exactly unchanged. Retained overlay corner coordinates, UVs and
weights must be unchanged; record hashes and exact omitted geometry/UVs.
Reject if more than 10 percent of overlay triangles would be omitted.

Moving the failed R3 blend intact to quarantine revealed relative source-image
links. Preserve approved absolute project-local image links when saving the
fresh production scene (relative_remap=False). A diagnostic may relink only
the exact SHA-verified image refs already bound to its charts in a derived
scene. Never modify the archived blend or substitute filename-matching art.

Bind the current facial projection helper, actual successful R2 diagnostic,
and independent limited cause review in the new exact plan. This correction
only permits one native S first-pose render after plan review. Facial seams,
likeness and all visual/motion gates remain unapproved.
