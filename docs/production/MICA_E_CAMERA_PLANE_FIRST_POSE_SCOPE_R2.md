# MICA E camera-plane first-pose scope R2

This scope replaces the failed R3 first-pose mechanism for one current MICA E
left-contact pose only. It uses the current approved E ImageGen green master and
its reviewed RGBA derivative. It does not authorize a motion clip, any other
direction, shooting, runtime packaging, HTML, a production pointer, or a Luna
test.

The R3 output is retained as `FAIL_NOT_PROMOTABLE`: its anatomical right calf
was rotated close to edge-on and fell from 79–110 source pixels to 4.95–9.28
render pixels. The new mechanism is a new source-preserving adapter, not a
rerender or relabel of that output.

The adapter takes the licensed Tripo action's E contact sample as the motion
translation and its fixed orthographic screen-space joint path. Each ImageGen
source-surface bone rotates only around the review camera normal. This retains
visible source-surface width instead of turning the right calf edge-on. The
left support lower-leg chain may move only along the camera ray so its evaluated
sole reaches the existing fixed neutral floor; this is not an actor-root floor
correction, limb-scale change, or claim that all 3D bone paths remain identical.

Before rendering, the adapter must bind the exact reviewed RGBA texture,
material graph, source surface and historical-neutral admission. It must record
and compare, before and after pose application, the actual source surface REST
matrices, UV loops, topology, vertex-group weights and RGBA bytes. It must also
prove the original Tripo screen-space bone-head path within 0.01 px, left support
clearance within 0.05 mm of the fixed R3 target, and the independent right-calf
cross-section witnesses retain at least 90% of their original width.

The one bounded output is a native 1920×1920 RGBA first-pose evidence image and
its same-process mesh preflight. It remains candidate-only until fresh
implementation and Ponytail FULL build-plan reviews, actual first-pose visual
reviews, and a first-pose seal all pass.
