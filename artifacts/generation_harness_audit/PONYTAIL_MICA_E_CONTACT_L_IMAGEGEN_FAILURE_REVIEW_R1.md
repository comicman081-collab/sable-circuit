## Why the second attempt did not fix the mechanism

The actual input order is identical in both requests: neutral appearance, standing true-E appearance, then a full generic rig guide. R2 only adds prose about an invisible baseline and placing the support sole at the lowest body point. R1 already has its forward sole as the lowest body point; flight images can satisfy that instruction too. This is a concrete non-discriminating criterion, not evidence that contact improved.

Neither request geometrically fixes the rendered MICA pelvis, scale and floor as a common reviewed coordinate system. The calibrated 2 mm guide result therefore does not automatically transfer into ImageGen pixels. R2 also restarts the entire character from neutral/profile inputs instead of preserving R1's already correct asymmetric limb relation. The outputs directly establish that the laterality text did not remain reliable under this full reauthoring. Dominance of the standing references over the motion reference is a plausible mechanism, not an observable fact about the image model's internals.

Its person, backpack, mechanical arms, bare feet and other visible pixels are forbidden appearance inputs. Do not composite, texture-project or locally redraw those pixels into MICA.

Keep the already correct anatomical plate placement, trailing limb identity, head identity, rifle/hands/main-barrel structure and E facing. 4. Replace the tautological lowest-point condition with a **predeclared common scale, pelvis/root reference and fixed ground baseline**, established from the reviewed guide and approved sprite layout before judging the returned image. Compare the returned visible heel/sole and joint chain against that reference. A modest heel-arrival pitch is legitimate; a rule demanding every contact foot be completely flat would incorrectly turn initial contact into down. Conversely, a bent/reaching pose must not pass solely because its heel is the lowest pixel.

After the one returned edit, independently check limb-marker continuity from thigh through boot **before** matting/packaging, then visible support against the fixed reference, costume devices, muzzle, and matte edges. Any reversed marker, unresolved support or missing device keeps the output non-promotable.
