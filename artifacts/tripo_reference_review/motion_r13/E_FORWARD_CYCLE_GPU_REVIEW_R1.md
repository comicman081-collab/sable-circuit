# Independent E forward-cycle and GPU diagnostic review

Verdict: **HOLD for grounded E locomotion and production**. The completed E r02 is an actual, distinct skeletal animation with preserved source artwork and repaired waist attachment. Its foot trajectory is not an acceptable grounded forward run yet. This is a substantive retargeting issue, not a request for coat polish.

I directly inspected original-scale samples 00, 12, 24 and 36 and all sixteen native battle-scale frames in chronological order. The latter were copied into a 1920×1080 board at exactly 1:1 pixel scale, with no render or resampling. The face, braid, two back devices, hands, rifle and upper torso remain coherent. The waist does not detach. The thin residual coat trim at the calf is not the blocker. The asymmetric leg excursion is visible even at the intended small battle size.

The independent read-only audit recomposed all 49 × 22 exported bone transforms and the visible skin. Its maximum joint difference from the recorded evaluated Blender joints is 0.0000015121 m. First/last global matrices match exactly, timestamps cover 0.625 s in 48 equal intervals, and the APNG actually decodes to 16 distinct frames totalling 625 ms. All 187 coincident front waist groups stay coincident over all 49 samples. This verifies export/playback fidelity and the torso repair; it does not establish gait quality.

I independently selected visible lower rubber strips in the original E source: anatomical RIGHT x165..267/y1470..1492 and LEFT x600..800/y1450..1477. The exact exported vertices and all per-sample results are retained in `e_cycle_readonly_r01/AUDIT.json`. Relative to the neutral minimum of these selected strips, RIGHT remains +0.023576..+0.671968 m and LEFT ranges −0.099243..+0.251881 m. These are deliberately narrow source-UV measurements, not certified 3D sole or floor authority; nevertheless the size and asymmetry agree with the visible failure. They cannot support a contact PASS.

The likely mechanism is `retarget_reviewed_direction_clip.py` retaining the target source pose's spread/bent rest axes while applying reference global rotation deltas: `R_pose * inverse(R_rest) * T_rest`. A reference's repaired sole contact is not invariant under that operation. Parent-relative translated heads then preserve target lengths but inherit the incorrect segment directions. The single mean leg-length ratio also cannot establish either foot's support constraint.

The bounded correction should transfer actual pose segment directions/joint goals in the declared view-aligned pelvis frame, map target rest axes onto those goals, and solve each target chain with its real lengths and a consistent knee plane. Shoe orientation and evaluated source-covered sole vertices need an explicit target calibration. Preserve the shared torso field, upper weapon pixels and source UVs. Do not manufacture contact with a per-frame root lift, global minimum floor or socket declarations. Verify the same E cycle with a fixed neutral floor and independently specified actor displacement before any motion promotion or further view motion batch.

I also inspected all eight native Godot GPU images: phases 0, .25, .5, .75, each before/at the first SOFT recoil sample. The source appearance and same-phase lower pose render, and the muzzle coordinates are visually consistent with the visible barrel end. The small upper recoil is present without a new waist opening. This is **one-view rendered association evidence only**: no independent pixel-exact barrel measurement, launched projectile, full recoil envelope, real Actor movement/collision, eight-by-eight movement/aim or live HTML was observed. The copied Godot code, exact inputs and RTX 4070 SUPER OpenGL log are bound in the review. Source-scale and battle-scale evidence are retained; a completed final quality gate is not inferred from container QA.

No production code, source art, old failed artifact or runtime pointer was changed. No Blender/render/generation/Luna job was started for this review. The only execution was a project-local read-only numeric/pixel audit and existing container validation.
