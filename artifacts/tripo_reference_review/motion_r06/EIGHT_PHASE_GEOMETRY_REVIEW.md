I recomputed phase classification from the 49 calibration rows with the exact contact contract; it returned all eight reported indices and no errors. All eight phase samples are distinct. For each, the guide image/frame equals the capture row, and the capture's actual sole arrays equal the calibration arrays. I recomputed the minimum sole clearance directly from those vertex arrays rather than trusting the reported clearance field; values match.

Existing independent native Blender reopen evidence in `motion_r04/NATIVE_MAPPING_QA.json` proved the same exact blend's REST and all 49 saved pose sole arrays match, with both sides weighted to their own named chains. That native probe was previously executed; this review did not launch Blender.

All six supporting phase minima remain at approximately 2 mm, below the 4 mm visible support limit. Same-side contact-to-down pelvis drops are 27.929 mm LEFT and 25.680 mm RIGHT, above the 15 mm requirement. The maximum sampled foot-axis yaw among these poses is approximately 0.272 degrees; the feet do not turn sideways. Passing travel-order differences cross sign as expected: LEFT-minus-RIGHT travel changes from +0.253 m at down_l to -0.029 m at passing_l, and from -0.153 m at down_r to +0.037 m at passing_r.

The original anchored retarget, calibration and capture hashes are unchanged. The existing leg-reach correction shortened LEFT support by one sample; it did not translate the root, lower the fixed floor or stretch a leg. Prior retarget evidence records zero reach clamp and micrometre-scale final support-anchor residuals. Those technical facts do not make this a full temporal no-slip or complete locomotion approval.

The native evidence validator passed all eight 1920×1080 containers. That verifies decoding/resolution only; this document's phase distinctions come from the direct image inspections and source-bound measurements above.
